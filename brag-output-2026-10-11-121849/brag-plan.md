# fitit. — launch video plan (vertical, 20 s)

Format: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 20.0 s (600 frames). Tone: `polished`, with a little playfulness.
Sources: `landing/` (screens, tokens, copy, images), `mobile/` (fitting-room curtain, Board / Try-on wording), `docs/design/welcome/` (rail photo).

## Answers before planning

- **What it is:** a wardrobe app that proposes complete outfits from the clothes you already own, for today's weather.
- **Who it is for:** anyone with a full closet and "nothing to wear".
- **What sets it apart:** it works from your own wardrobe (you photograph a piece once; the background is removed and it is described for you), and you see the outfit two ways: laid flat on a Board, and as an AI try-on revealed by a fitting-room curtain.
- **Strongest moment:** the curtain drawing aside on the try-on picture.
- **Visual hook:** a tidy, full clothing rail under the line "A full closet. Nothing to wear."
- **Real UI shown:** Add clothes (photo → cut-out → label) → Wardrobe grid → Today (weather + board) → Outfit view with the Fitting room curtain and the try-on picture.
- **Tone:** calm, editorial, expensive; quick enough for TikTok.
- **Share caption:** "A full closet and nothing to wear? fitit. dresses you from the clothes you already own."

## Angle

Open on the feeling everyone knows, answer it with the name, then show the loop in three plain steps on the real screens: snap it once, get dressed for the weather, see it on you.

- **Hook:** "A full closet. *Nothing* to wear."
- **Highlights:** Snap it once. / Dressed for the weather. / See it on you.
- **Punchline:** "fitit." + "Your closet, dressed for today."

## Decisions

- The only real try-on picture in the project shows a white tee, blue jeans and white sneakers, so the weather and the board use the landing's matching state: 24°, clear and warm, "Easy everyday". The board and the try-on therefore show the same outfit; nothing is faked.
- Outfit views are named Board and Try-on only (no collage, no "on figure").
- No store badges, no prices, no numbers, no "coming to" line (the landing says "made for iPhone and Android", not "coming to").
- TikTok safe zones: captions and key UI stay inside y 220–1500 and x ≤ 940. The lower part of the phone runs into the bottom zone on purpose; nothing there needs to be read.

## Visual identity

Porcelain #FAFAF7, surface #FFFFFF, ink #17201C, secondary #5A635E, hairline #D9DED6, well #E6EBE3, bottle green #2F5D4C.
Bodoni Moda Medium (+ Medium Italic for one green accent word per headline), Figtree 400/500/600. Small uppercase labels, care-label tags, hairlines, wells.

## Sound

Synthesised in numpy, D major, 108 bpm: soft electric-piano chords (Dmaj9 – Bm7 – Gmaj7 – A6/9), a quiet pluck arpeggio, sub bass, soft kick and a brushed off-beat. Effects are tuned to the key and mixed under the music with the same reverb: a shutter tick on the snap, a filtered whoosh as the background falls away, three plucks as the pieces land, a fabric swish for the curtain, and a D major resolve with a bell on the outro. Cuts land on beats.
